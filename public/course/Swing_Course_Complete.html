1Mindset, Market Reality & Scam Protection

1.1 The 99% vs 1% Reality

In the stock market, 99% of people lose their hard-earned money; only ~1% consistently win. Three parties exist:

  • Retailers/Losers (99%) — give away earned savings
  • Winners: Operators (move price with power) + Smart Money (businessman mindset — buy cheap, sell dear)
  • Third Party: News channels, YouTube/Telegram "tip" scammers, panic retailers — they help operators profit

1.3 Value vs Momentum

Trade value, not momentum. Buffett principle: "Buy when everyone is selling; sell when everyone is buying." "Buy high, sell higher / trend is your friend / ride the rising train" — all lies. The train only stops at the right station = the right price level.

1.4 Operator vs Promoter

  • Promoter = company owner (e.g. Ambani) — grows company profit
  • Operator = manipulates share price (Harshad Mehta era) — can take ₹20 stock to ₹1000
  • Goldman Sachs = "official operator" — says "buy" at tops, "sell" at bottoms

6.2 RHS / Cup-with-Handle

7Fundamental Analysis + 67-Funda

7.1 Core Ratios

RatioFormulaGood
Market CapPrice × Total Shares—
PEPrice ÷ EPS15–20; >60 avoid
ROCEEBIT ÷ Capital Employed ×10020%+ legendary, 12%+ ok
ROE (banks)Net Profit ÷ Equity ×10012%+ ok, 15%+ great

7.2 Financial Statements

  • P&L: Sales → OPM (OP÷Sales×100) → Net Profit. Ignore one-time "other income".
  • Balance Sheet: Assets = Liabilities always. Reserves rising = profit. Expanding fixed assets + profit dip = OK (expansion).
  • Cash Flow: Operating cash must be strong.
  • Shareholding: Promoter 50%+, powerful players 25%+ = good. Public <15% = control.
  • Debt & Pledge: NDE <0.2. POC = Pledge% × Promoter% ÷ 100. POC <5% OK, 5–15% caution, 15%+ avoid.
  • Debtor Days: Receivables ÷ Sales × 365. Smaller = better.
  • PB: <0.4 = reversion trade (0.8 target = 40–50% potential).

7.3 67-Funda (10-Point Screen)

🔟 The 67-Funda 10-Point Checklist

  1. Company down ≥67% from its TOP (2/3 value wipeout; 35% not enough)
  2. Fall reason is clear (sentiment/business/fundamental)
  3. That reason no longer exists
  4. Proven track record
  5. NOT a loss-making / disgraced company
  6. Quarterly sales + profit improvement
  7. ≥100% profit potential (current → ATH/2nd-high)
  8. 100% in 1 yr → book
  9. Not in 1 yr → wait for ATH or 3x
  10. Blessings/luck
Entry: screener (Net Profit ₹50cr+, PE vs median, D/E, ROCE sort) → after quarterly improvement → target = improvement-price to 100%. Exit: book at 100% (partial OK), exit at 33% dip = "loser".

9.4 Put Selling — Seller Math (DAB example)

DAB ₹510, want ₹500. Sell 500 put @ ₹2.
Stays ₹510 → expire worthless → +₹2
Falls to ₹500 → ATM → +₹2 (no delivery)
Falls to ₹450 → buy @500, intrinsic 50 − 50 + 2 = −₹48 ×1250 = −₹60,000
EFFECTIVE BUY = 500 − 2 = ₹498 ("chit bhi meri, pat bhi meri")

9.5 Roll Over (postpone losses)

Month-end: buy back + sell next month SAME strike. Keep rolling while liquidity exists. Square off 2 days before expiry.
