Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Institutional Stock Audit?

An institutional stock audit measures an asset against 100 quantitative data points including fundamental safety, debt leverage, valuation percentiles, and institutional demand floors to assign a conviction score out of 100.

How does ABCD Tranche Laddering work?

Instead of allocating 100% capital at one price, the ABCD Ladder divides buying into four tranches A B C and D triggered by historical pullback milestones.
